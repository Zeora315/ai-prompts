# Quick Start

Welcome to **AI Prompts**! This site collects curated AI prompts, organized into "Work & Efficiency / Content Creation / Academic & Pro / Others" to help you drive AI more effectively.

## Use a prompt in three steps

1. **Copy**: Open the relevant category and click the copy button at the top-right of the prompt code block.
2. **Replace placeholders**: Replace the `{{...}}` tokens (e.g. `{{meeting notes}}`, `{{topic}}`) with your real content.
3. **Paste & send**: Paste the filled-in prompt into any AI chat tool (ChatGPT, Claude, Tongyi Qianwen, etc.) and send it.

::::tip
The `{{...}}` placeholders are only illustrative. You can keep, delete, or rewrite them freely to adjust the wording and requirements to your needs.
::::

## Tips

- **Define the role and output format**: Prompts already specify a role (e.g. "senior executive assistant") and a format (list / table), which usually works better.
- **Add constraints**: Append requirements at the end, e.g. "output as a table", "within 100 words", "explain for beginners".
- **Iterate**: If the first version isn't satisfying, ask the AI to "rewrite in another style" or "make it more professional" to approach the ideal result step by step.
- **Save frequent combos**: Bookmark high-frequency prompts in your notes to build a personal prompt library.
