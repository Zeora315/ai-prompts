# KimChat Paper Prompt Model Manual

A collection of AI prompts for "KimChat Paper Prompt Model Manual", suited to academic research and study. Copy the prompts below to your AI and补充 your specific requirements as needed.

## Prompt

```text
KimChat Paper Prompt Model Manual

1. Literature review prompt
Please write a literature review on (research topic). My thesis direction is XXXX. I have found the following papers: (title, author, year, abstract, keywords for paper 1) (title, author, year, abstract, keywords for paper 2) ... (paper n). Can you write a ~ (word count) literature review based on these? Structure it as: Introduction (background, significance, purpose, scope); Body (organize, analyze, and evaluate the literature by theme/method/viewpoint); Conclusion (summarize main findings, contributions, and gaps).

2. Polishing & revision prompt
Please help me polish and revise the following sentence from my paper ( ). Can you improve its language, logic, or content quality? Give the revised version and explain your changes and suggestions.
Template: "This paper proposes a method that uses reinforcement learning to learn from humans for training chatbots. It uses self-dialogue and user feedback to generate diverse, informative conversations for fine-tuning. Evaluated on two dialogue tasks, it improves fluency, consistency, and engagement."

3. Topic selection prompt
I am a graduate student in (major), researching (direction). You are my supervisor. I'm interested in (tech area) but haven't formed a concrete research question. Scholars have explored (area); I want to start from there. Recommend 10 reference topics.
Template: sociology / family sociology / urban Chinese parents asking grandparents to help raise kids.

4. Outline prompt
I want to write a paper on (topic), direction XXXXX. Can you draft an outline with: Title, Abstract, Introduction, Methods, Experiments, Results, Discussion, Conclusion, References.

5. Theory prompt
I am a graduate student in (major), direction (xx); you are my supervisor. My chosen topic is (xx). Recommend suitable research theories from the (xx) perspective, with name, author, origin, and main viewpoint.

6. Acknowledgements prompt
Write ~ (word count) acknowledgements for my paper titled (xxxx), supervisor (xxxx), collaborators (xxxx). Thank: (person 1) for (help); (person 2) for (help); (person 3) for (help). Use polite, grateful tone with proper formatting.

7. References prompt
I want to cite literature to support my arguments and methods on (topic). Recommend papers with title, author, year, abstract, and keywords.

8. Overall logic polishing
First give KimChat an identity: "You are a paper-optimization expert with deep knowledge of writing norms, literature review, data analysis, and research methodology. Your task is to help authors improve paper quality — structure, language, figures — and give targeted advice in a professional tone." Then ask: "Can you optimize my paper structure and content? Here is my structure: 1. Introduction 1.1 Background 1.2 Problem 2. Related Work 2.1 Overview 2.2 Results 3. Dataset 3.1 Source 3.2 Preprocessing." For content: "What key points should I consider when discussing (term)?" For sentences: "How should I revise this sentence to meet academic norms, and why?"

9. Overall optimization prompt
Topic selection: "Hello, I'm a (discipline) student writing a paper on (topic) but don't know the direction. Any suggestions?" Example: CS student / AI paper.

Paper GPT prompt collection:
1. Literature review  2. Polishing  3. Topic selection  4. Outline  5. Theory  6. Acknowledgements  7. References  8. Logic polishing  9. Overall optimization  10. Proposal defense  11. 22 KimChat reading prompts  12. English paper writing & polishing  13. Strongest review prompt
```

## Usage tips

- Copy the prompt above in full and send it to the AI.
- Replace any `( )` placeholders or examples with your own content.
- If the output isn't satisfying, add style/length/platform preferences for iteration.
